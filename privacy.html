Privacy Policy

Learn how Nirali infra and Capetown Heights collect, handle, and safeguard your personal information when you connect with us.

Last Updated: November 2026

1. Our Commitment to Your Privacy

At CAPETOWN HEIGHTS and Nirali infra, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data you share with us. This Privacy Policy details our practices concerning data collection, storage, processing, and disclosure for all prospective homebuyers, investors, and website visitors.

Key Transparency Guarantee: We do not sell, rent, monetize, or lease your personal contact details to external marketing agencies or unsolicited telecallers. Data provided to us is used strictly to assist you with Capetown Heights property information and site visits.

2. Information We Collect

When you interact with our website or contact our sales team, we may collect the following categories of information:

  • Contact Details: Your full name, telephone / mobile number, email address, and residential city.
  • Project Preferences: Interested configuration (1 BHK, 2 BHK, 3 BHK, or commercial shops), investment budget range, and preferred timeframe for possession.
  • Enquiry Details: Specific questions, booking requests, site visit date preferences, or loan assistance requirements submitted via our contact forms.
  • Technical Browsing Data: Aggregated, non-personally identifiable technical information such as device type, browser version, operating system, and pages visited, collected through standard web server logs.

3. How We Use Collected Information

Your information is utilized solely for legitimate real estate business communication and customer support purposes, including:

  • Responding directly to your property inquiries and providing comprehensive project brochures, pricing breakdowns, and floor plan documents.
  • Scheduling and coordinating physical site visits with our on-ground project sales representatives at Ultan Faliya Road, Silvassa.
  • Assisting you with home loan eligibility calculations and coordinating with our approved banking partners (SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Bank of Baroda, Axis Bank) only upon your explicit request.
  • Providing important statutory updates, construction milestone progress, and verified promotional announcements regarding Capetown Heights.

4. Information Security & Data Storage

Nirali infra implements robust technical and administrative security controls to safeguard customer information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or accidental destruction.

  • Our website utilizes encrypted SSL (Secure Sockets Layer) connections for secure data transmission between your browser and our servers.
  • Customer enquiry records and contact submissions are restricted to authorized sales managers and administrative personnel with verified credentials.

5. Sharing of Information with Authorized Third Parties

We maintain strict confidentiality standards and share information only under specific, limited circumstances:

  • Banking & Financial Partners: If you request home loan consultation or bank assistance, your necessary contact information may be shared with authorized loan officers from our empanelled banks (SBI, HDFC, ICICI, etc.) to evaluate eligibility.
  • Legal & Regulatory Authorities: We may disclose information if mandated by applicable law, court orders, or statutory regulatory bodies in Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu.

6. Cookies & Analytics

Our website uses lightweight functional cookies and standard analytics tools (such as Google Analytics) to understand website visitor trends, optimize page loading performance, and improve mobile browsing experience.

You can modify your browser settings to decline cookies at any time; however, certain visual features or interactive calculators on the website may have reduced functionality.

7. Your Rights & Opt-Out Options

You retain complete control over your personal data:

  • You may request a copy of the personal details we hold regarding your inquiries.
  • You may request corrections, updates, or complete deletion of your contact records from our communication lists.
  • You can opt-out from phone calls, WhatsApp messages, or promotional emails at any time by notifying our support desk.
