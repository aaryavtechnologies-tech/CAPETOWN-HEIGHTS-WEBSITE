Introducing Capetown Heights

Capetown Heights is an authentic real estate development project strategically situated in Silvassa, Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu. Developed by Nirali infra, the project embodies modern infrastructure, comprehensive lifestyle amenities, and prime connectivity.

Strategic Location

Conveniently located on Ultan Faliya Road, Police Line Road, offering direct connectivity across Silvassa.

Verified Amenities

From double basement parking and AC banquet facilities to multi-tier gardens and dedicated sports zones.

Modern Security

Equipped with CCTV surveillance, video door security systems, and digital lock access for complete peace of mind.

Connectivity

Seamless road connectivity with swift access to key commercial hubs, shopping markets, schools, hospitals, and transit routes across Silvassa.

Amenities
